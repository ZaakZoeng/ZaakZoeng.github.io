Successfully passed my doctoral dissertation defense! 😊

April 22, 2026

2026

The Ph.D. defense is a major milestone in my research career and also a brand new beginning.

On April 22, 2026, I successfully defended my doctoral dissertation, bringing a successful conclusion to my five years of graduate study. All the hard work, countless revisions, and late‑night experiments over the past five years became immensely worthwhile the moment the defense committee announced “pass.” I am deeply grateful to my advisor for the meticulous guidance, to my labmates for their generous help, and to my family and friends for their unwavering support.

2026年4月22日，我顺利通过博士论文答辩，为五年的研究生学习画上了圆满的句号。过去五年里所有的辛勤付出、反复修改、熬夜实验，在答辩委员会宣布“通过”的那一刻，都变得无比值得。我由衷感谢导师的悉心指导，感谢同学们的鼎力相助，也感谢家人朋友一路以来的支持。
